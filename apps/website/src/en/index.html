BEFORE YOUR FIRST SHOT

A few things
to know.

Does Jingxu train its own video model?+

Jingxu focuses on production, shot revisions and delivery. Generation comes from your configured provider. You can also import existing media.

Is character consistency guaranteed?+

No. References help guide generation, but every result needs review. Jingxu keeps references and candidate history connected so you can make a specific revision.

Can I try it without an API key?+

Use clearly labeled demo illustrations or import your own images and videos. Online generation requires an enabled model, key and provider credits.

Does mobile require a workstation?+

No. Flutter mobile keeps projects, assets and editing on the device. AI requests go directly to your configured provider. Keep the app open during local video export; background execution depends on the operating system.

Where do my media and keys go?+

Projects stay on your device by default. Necessary prompts and references are sent to your selected provider for generation. Device secure storage protects mobile API keys; keys are excluded from project packages.
