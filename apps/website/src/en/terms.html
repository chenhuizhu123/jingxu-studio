Development preview usage

Updated November 4, 2026.

Preview status

Jingxu Studio is in development. Website illustrations and interactions demonstrate workflows, not generation quality. Platform build and testing status follows actual validation records. An unsigned build is not a signed public release.

Generation and fees

You choose and configure third-party providers. Their models, access rules, credits and pricing determine generation costs and output. References do not guarantee consistent faces or scenes in every shot. Preview results before delivery.

Editing and exports

The preview includes local media assembly, candidates, storyboards and subtitle exports. Read current export descriptions for audio and caption behavior. Shot-timed subtitles are not automatic speech alignment. Planned features are not delivered features.

Pricing

Payments are not enabled. The proposed Pro price is a research hypothesis, not an available subscription or payment commitment. Model-provider charges are separate.

Your content

Use scripts, images, videos, voices and music you have permission to use. Review platform rules, rights and factual claims before publishing. The product does not guarantee views, sales or revenue.

Backups

Keep independent backups of important source media and project packages. Formal commercial terms must be completed by the operating entity before launch.
